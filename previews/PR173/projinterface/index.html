Defining projective transformations

See Projective transformations for what projective transformations are and why they are treated specially.

Transformations

The abstract type DataAugmentation.ProjectiveTransform represents a projective transformation. A ProjectiveTransform needs to implement DataAugmentation.getprojection(tfm, bounds; randstate) that should return a Transformation from CoordinateTransformations.jl.

To apply a fixed projection P that does not depend on the bounds, wrap it in Project(P).

Items

To add support for projective transformations to an item I, you need to implement

  • DataAugmentation.getbounds(item::I) returns the spatial bounds of the item; and
  • DataAugmentation.project(P, item::I, indices) applies the projective transformation P and crops to indices

To support apply!-ing projective transformations, DataAugmentation.project!(bufitem, P, item, indices) can also be implemented.
